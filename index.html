EN pantograph gauge (A.3.12) (mm)

Po (head raised to 6,5 m) and Pu (5,0 m) shall be ≤ 0, on straight track, at R = 150 m and at the radius where variable stops begin; formulae A.66–A.75 between the pivots (stationary on canted track), A.76–A.85 beyond them (running with cant deficiency), the set for s ≤ 0,225 or s > 0,225. Worst case shown; all cases in the export.

EN bogie parts (A.3.11) (all bogie types, mm)

Formulae of independent-axle vehicles A.56–A.59 with w = 0 and a = distance between the end axles. KE half-width = smallest over the part's height range (lowered by wheel wear and, on the frame, the primary travel; raised by the primary rebound).

EN 15273-2:2009 static gauge method (A.2) (reference profile and rules on the Customer KE & SOD tab)

Reductions A.2 (between the bogie centres) and A.4 (beyond) with Δ not below 7,5 m², no roll term; the vehicle may use a static gauge only when its flexibility coefficient does not exceed smax (A.2.3.1). Margin = reference half-width − E − profile half-width; the profile top must stay within the reference top. Lower parts up to 0,43 m: height − wheel wear − total suspension displacement ≥ lower limit (A.2.3.2).

Sway (roll) test readings

Vehicle on straight, level track; shims of height D under the wheels of the low side. One row per shim step: lateral body displacement in the track plane (perpendicular to the shimmed rail plane) at 2 or 3 heights above the rail, relative to the reading without shims, positive towards the low rail. Paste rows from Excel.

Sway test evaluation (least squares y = s·D/L·(h − hc))

Accepted when the measured roll displacement per 100 mm of cant is not larger than the one used in gauging at h1, h2 and the top of the RS profiles.

Roll displacement per 100 mm cant

Kinematic gauge test plan

Reproduced on the test stand: wheel–rail play, primary and secondary lateral play pushed to the stops (× A), and the roll by shims = cant + wind equivalent cant. The test template is the customer KE minus the terms that the test does not reproduce (overthrow at Rs, rail wear, widening, tolerances, random roll, upward movement).
